*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

FoodZip is a food barcode scanner. This policy explains what happens with
information when you use the app.

## Summary

- FoodZip has **no accounts, no ads and no analytics**.
- We (the developer) **do not collect, store or sell any personal data**.
- Your scan history stays **on your device**.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only to read barcodes while the scanner is open.
Camera images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent
anywhere.

## Product lookups

To show information about a product, the app sends the product's barcode
number to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, open
database of food products. Only the barcode is sent — no name, account,
location or other personal information. Like any website, Open Food Facts
receives your device's IP address with the request; their own
[privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy) applies to it.

Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

## Data stored on your device

The list of products you checked (history) is saved locally on your device so
you can open them again. You can delete single items (swipe left) or everything with **Clear** in the
app, or by uninstalling the app. It is not backed up or sent to us.

## Links

Some screens open external websites (for example Wikipedia articles about
food additives). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

FoodZip does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Health information

FoodZip shows information from public databases to help you compare products.
It is not medical or dietary advice. If you have allergies or a health
condition, always check the product label.
