*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

FoodZip is a food barcode scanner for iPhone (and later Android). This policy
explains what happens with information when you use the app.

## Summary

- FoodZip has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs**.
- The developer **does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data**.
  There is no FoodZip server.
- Your scan history stays **on your device** until you delete it.
- To look up a product, the app sends **only the barcode number** to Open Food
  Facts.

## Who we are

FoodZip is developed and published by **Siarhei Hubanau**, an individual
developer based in Vilnius, Lithuania (EU). For the purposes of the EU General
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), he is the controller of any personal data
processed by the app.

Contact: **support@foodzip.app**

## Camera

The app uses the camera only while the scanner screen is open, to read
barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device and are never saved or
sent anywhere.

Barcode recognition uses:

- **iPhone:** Apple's built-in Vision framework, which works fully on the
  device.
- **Android:** Google ML Kit. ML Kit recognises barcodes on the device and does
  not send images or barcode contents to Google. Like other Google SDKs, it may
  send Google technical information about the device, the app and the
  performance of the scanner (for example the device model, OS version and
  error rates). See the
  [ML Kit terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

## Product lookups

To show information about a product, the app sends the product's barcode
number to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, open
database of food products run by a non-profit association in France. Only the
barcode is sent — no name, account, location or other personal information.
Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address with the
request and processes it under its own
[privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy). The app identifies
itself to Open Food Facts as `FoodZip/version (support@foodzip.app)`, as they
request; this does not identify you.

Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

## Data stored on your device

The list of products you checked (history) is saved locally on your device so
you can open them again. It contains product barcodes, names, brands and scores.
It is not sent to the developer.

## Data retention

- **History:** kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete single
  items (swipe left) or everything with **Clear** in the app. Uninstalling the
  app deletes it too.
- **Camera images:** not kept at all.
- **Email to support:** if you write to us, your message and email address are
  kept only as long as needed to answer you, and at most 12 months.
- The developer keeps no logs and no other data about you.

## Apple and Google

FoodZip is distributed through the App Store (and later Google Play). Apple and
Google process data about app downloads, purchases and app stability under
their own privacy policies. If you have allowed it in your device settings
(on iPhone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements →
Share with App Developers**), Apple may give the developer anonymous crash
reports and usage statistics. Google Play may provide similar anonymous
statistics (Android vitals). These reports do not identify you, and the
developer uses them only to fix bugs.

## Links

Some screens open external websites (for example Wikipedia articles about
food additives). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Your privacy rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- restrict or object to its processing;
- data portability;
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Lithuania this is the
  State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija,
  [vdai.lrv.lt](https://vdai.lrv.lt)); you can also contact the authority in
  your own EU country.

Because the developer does not hold personal data about app users, most
requests can be fulfilled directly in the app (delete history) or by
uninstalling it. For anything else — including emails you sent us — write to
**support@foodzip.app** and we will answer within one month.

## Children

FoodZip is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children.

## Health information

FoodZip shows information from public databases to help you compare products.
It is not medical or dietary advice. If you have allergies or a health
condition, always check the product label.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at
[foodzip.app/privacy](https://foodzip.app/privacy/) with a new date.
